Privacy policy.

How we handle the information you send us.

Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

When you contact Day's Mobile Mechanics & Auto Repair, we collect what you choose to send: your name, phone number, email, city or ZIP code, vehicle details and a description of the problem. The work order form linked on this site is provided by ShopMonkey, the system we use to manage quotes and repairs, so anything you send through it is stored there.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your message, give you a price, schedule the work and keep a record of the repair. We may call or text you about the job you asked about. If you check the text message box on the work order form, we may text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the services that help us run the business, such as ShopMonkey and our website host, and only as much as they need to do that job.

Website analytics

This website may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. These tools may use cookies to count visits and see which pages are useful. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information we have about you by emailing dmmechanics@yahoo.com or calling 817-709-1357.
